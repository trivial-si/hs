# Code of conduct

We want people with different experience and backgrounds to feel welcome contributing to Trivial.si projects.

Be considerate and specific. Critique ideas and code, explain your reasoning, give credit, and respect boundaries. Help newcomers find the next useful step. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces used to represent the project.

## Reporting and response

Report conduct concerns privately to Ivan Filković at **ivan.filkovic@gmail.com**. Include links or other context when possible. Please do not publish another person's private information in a public issue.

The maintainer will review reports discreetly, sharing details only as needed to respond. Responses may include a clarification, warning, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent restriction on participation, depending on severity and repetition. A reporter or affected participant may ask for reconsideration through the same private contact. If the concern involves the maintainer, state that explicitly; an independent reviewer will be sought where possible. The project currently has one maintainer.
